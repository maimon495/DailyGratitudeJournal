Common questions

Where are my entries stored?
On your iPhone, and only on your iPhone. Entries are never uploaded to a server. That also means they aren't backed up to us — if you delete the app, they're gone.

How do I turn the daily reminder on or off?
Open Settings in the app and use the Daily Reminder toggle. If nothing happens, check that notifications are allowed for the app in the iPhone's own Settings.

How do I delete my account?
Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and the journal entries on your device, and cannot be undone. If you're asked to sign in again first, that's a security requirement — sign out, sign back in, and try once more.

Why do I see ads?
Ads keep the app free. You can decline tracking when asked, or later in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking — you'll still see ads, but they won't be personalised.

I'm in the EU/UK and want to change my ad consent.
Settings → Ad Privacy Settings in the app reopens the consent screen.
