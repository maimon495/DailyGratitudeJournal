Privacy Policy

Daily Gratitude Journal · Last updated 6 September 2026

This policy explains what Daily Gratitude Journal ("the app") collects, why, and what choices you have. The app is published by sgtpepper development.

Your journal entries stay on your device

The app does not upload, transmit, or store your journal entries on any server. Everything you write is saved locally on your iPhone using Apple's on-device storage. We cannot read your entries. If you delete the app, your entries are deleted with it.

Account information

Signing in is handled by Firebase Authentication, a service from Google. When you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive and store:

  • Your email address
  • Your display name, if you choose to share it
  • A unique account identifier

This is used only to sign you in and keep your settings associated with your account. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email, we only ever see Apple's private relay address.

Advertising

The app displays banner advertisements through Google AdMob, which keeps the app free. To serve ads, Google may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA)
  • General device information, such as model and operating system version
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Interactions with the ads shown

On first launch the app asks, through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, whether you allow tracking. If you decline, the advertising identifier is not used and you will see non-personalised ads instead. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your iPhone.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you will also see a consent screen from Google letting you choose how your data is used for advertising. You can reopen those choices at any time from Settings → Ad Privacy Settings inside the app.

Google's handling of this data is described in Google's privacy policy.

Analytics

The app uses Firebase Analytics to understand general usage — which screens are opened, how often the app is launched, and whether it crashes. This is reported in aggregate and is not used to identify you personally. It never includes the content of your journal entries.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device. Your chosen reminder time is stored on the device only and is never sent anywhere.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account. Open Settings → Delete Account in the app. This permanently deletes your account and the journal entries stored on your device. It cannot be undone.
  • Turn off tracking. Decline the tracking prompt, or change it later in iOS Settings as described above.
  • Turn off notifications. Use the toggle in the app's Settings, or iOS Settings.
  • If you are covered by the GDPR or CCPA, you may request access to or deletion of the account information described above by contacting us.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date at the top.
